\textbf{Declarations}

\textbf{Funding.} This study was conducted without external funding.

\textbf{Competing interests.} The author declares no competing interests relevant to this study.

\textbf{Ethics approval.} Not required: this is a model-based economic evaluation using previously published
aggregate trial and literature data; no human participants, human tissue or identifiable personal data were involved.

\textbf{Consent to participate / for publication.} Not applicable.

\textbf{Data availability.} All data used in this analysis are derived from published sources cited in the references.
The full model-selection results (15{,}625 distribution combinations) and all model outputs are available in the
project repository at \url{https://github.com/yichao2022/cea-intismeran}, including
\texttt{output/survival\_combos\_15625.csv} and \texttt{output/canonical\_results.json}.

\textbf{Code availability.} The partitioned survival model was implemented in Python~3.12 and is available at
\url{https://github.com/yichao2022/cea-intismeran} (tag \texttt{v1.0-submission}).
Running \texttt{python3 results\_pipeline.py} reproduces every reported quantity (base case, decomposition,
scenarios, one-way deterministic sensitivity analysis, price ladder and analytic threshold prices) from a single
canonical model implementation, with algebraic consistency checks enforced before export;
adding \texttt{-\/-psa} reproduces the probabilistic analysis.

\textbf{Author contributions.} Yichao Jin conceived and designed the study, developed the model, conducted the
analysis, and wrote the manuscript.

\textbf{Acknowledgments.} None.

\textbf{Reporting standard.} This study adheres to the Consolidated Health Economic Evaluation Reporting Standards
2022 (CHEERS 2022; Husereau et al., \textit{Value in Health} 2022); the completed checklist is provided in the
supplementary appendix.
