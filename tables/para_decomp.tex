The \$200,000 acquisition cost of intismeran contributed substantially to the incremental cost. Under the corrected general-population mortality inputs, the combination arm had \$16,184 higher locoregional-recurrence management costs (\$86,330 vs \$70,146) but \$70,365 lower distant-metastasis management costs (\$358,736 vs \$429,100) than the \mbox{pembrolizumab}-alone arm, because \mbox{pembrolizumab}-alone patients spend more time in the DM state over the lifetime (1.639 vs 1.370 discounted DM QALYs). The incremental QALY gain of 4.051 was driven primarily by additional time spent in the recurrence-free state (4.032 RF QALYs gained), with a modest additional 0.288 LR QALYs, partly offset by a 0.269-QALY reduction in the DM state.
