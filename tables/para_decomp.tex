The \$200,000 acquisition cost of intismeran contributed substantially to the incremental cost. Under the corrected general-population mortality inputs, the combination arm had \$20,527 higher locoregional-recurrence management costs (\$93,867 vs \$73,340) but \$39,298 lower distant-metastasis management costs (\$407,465 vs \$446,762) than the \mbox{pembrolizumab}-alone arm, because \mbox{pembrolizumab}-alone patients spend more time in the DM state over the lifetime (1.706 vs 1.556 discounted DM QALYs). The incremental QALY gain of 4.653 was driven primarily by additional time spent in the recurrence-free state (4.438 RF QALYs gained), with a modest additional 0.365 LR QALYs, partly offset by a 0.150-QALY reduction in the DM state.
